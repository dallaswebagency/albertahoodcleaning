Our cleaning process

Our professional hood cleaning process begins on the roof-top where the fan unit is located and ends at the bottom on the kitchen floor. Scroll through all 19 steps and follow the job through your building.

  1. 1

    Remove all filters from hood and soak.

    Hood
  2. 2

    Cover cooking line equipment with heavy plastic protection.

    Kitchen
  3. 3

    Funnel plastic into bucket.

    Hood
  4. 4

    Partially remove and clean roof fan with chemical degreasers and high pressure hot water.

    Roof
  5. 5

    Scrape and clean duct with chemical and high pressure hot water.

    Duct
  6. 6

    Return roof fan, check belts and secure.

    Roof
  7. 7

    Clean grease box, overflow and roof-top work area.

    Roof
  8. 8

    Hood cleaning, plenum, filters and back splash with chemical and high pressure hot water.

    Hood
  9. 9

    Collect and remove grease mixture from bucket.

    Hood
  10. 10

    Remove funnel plastic.

    Hood
  11. 11

    Return and install filters.

    Hood
  12. 12

    Pressure wash kitchen floor work areas.

    Kitchen
  13. 13

    Vacuum and mop excess on floor.

    Kitchen
  14. 14

    Dry and apply polish to stainless steel areas.

    Kitchen
  15. 15

    Completely clean work areas, equipment, counters and floors.

    Kitchen
  16. 16

    Re-light the pilot light.

    Kitchen
  17. 17

    Review the status of the cleaning project checklist with the manager on duty.

    Sign-off
  18. 18

    Attach certification sticker to hood.

    Sign-off
  19. 19

    Before and after photos.
